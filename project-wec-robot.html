Autonomous Block-Sorting Robot

SFU Representative — Western Engineering Competition, University of Alberta
Arduino Robotics Autonomous Systems Remote Control Rapid Prototyping

Objective

To represent Simon Fraser University in the Senior Design category at the Western Engineering Competition, hosted at the University of Alberta, designing and building a working Arduino-based robot entirely on-site within an 8-hour build window, using a fixed starting kit plus an additional materials budget to source extra parts.

Result

Built a complete robot from the ground up in 8 hours on-site, capable of picking up three different sizes of blocks and sorting them into two to three differently sized containers, then picking up and delivering those containers to separate scoring locations for points.

Remote-Controlled & Autonomous Modes

The competition required two distinct operating modes: a remote-controlled mode for direct manual operation, and a fully autonomous mode that had to complete the same block-sorting and delivery task on its own. Both modes were built out and the autonomous mode competed head-to-head against other teams' entries.

Building Under Time Pressure

Unlike a project developed over weeks, this robot was designed, built, wired, and coded entirely within a single 8-hour window at the competition venue, starting from a fixed kit of parts and a limited budget for anything extra — a test of fast, practical engineering decision-making as much as technical skill.
