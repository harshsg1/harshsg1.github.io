Municipal Water-to-Energy Generator

1st Place — SFU Engineering Senior Design Competition
Energy Harvesting Water Conservation Renewable Energy Mechanical Design

Objective

To design and build a working device that generates electricity from a standard municipal water supply — such as a garden hose — with the dual goals of water conservation and energy efficiency, using only the limited kit of resources provided to every team in the competition.

Result

The device took 1st place at the SFU Engineering Senior Design Competition and was recognized as the most energy-efficient and water-conservative design entered, built entirely within the fixed resource constraints given to all competitors.

Constraints & Approach

Every team in the competition worked from the same limited set of provided components, so the result came down to how efficiently those resources were used rather than access to better parts. The design focused on converting the flow and pressure of a standard municipal water line — the kind found on any household hose — directly into usable electrical output, while minimizing water waste in the process.
